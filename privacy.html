Privacy Policy

Chick Again for iPhone. Effective 13 October 2026.

English

Who I am

Chick Again is made by Ahmet Kazankaya, an independent developer. Contact: kasparovabi@gmail.com.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress, chosen character and settings (music, sounds, vibration) on your device only. I do not run any server, there is no account, and I do not receive this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising and vendor identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the settings. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, so your advertising identifier is not shared with AdMob unless you allowed tracking for this app in iOS settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your payment details; the game only learns whether the purchase is active so it can turn off between-level ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, use the controls described in Google's policies or contact me and I will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Yasanın gerektirdiği yerlerde (örneğin AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre) oyun, kişiselleştirilmiş reklam göstermeden önce Google'ın onay formuyla iznini ister; kararını istediğin zaman ayarlardaki Gizlilik seçenekleri'nden değiştirebilirsin. Google'ın bu verileri nasıl kullandığı Google'ın hizmetlerini kullanan sitelerden ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı sayfasında ve Google Gizlilik Politikası'nda anlatılıyor.
